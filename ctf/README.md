# CTF Activity

I compete in CTFs that mirror real blue-team work rather than general exploitation challenges, since that reflects the direction I'm building my career in — forensics, threat hunting, and detection engineering.

## HackTheBox Holmes CTF 2026

**17–21 September 2026** · Team **0xnobody** · **Ranked 837th of 5,637 teams (top 15%)** · 18/111 challenges solved · 1,625 points

This one's entirely defensive — no exploitation, no gaining access to boxes. Every challenge is a case file: you're handed memory dumps, packet captures, or log sets and have to reconstruct what an attacker actually did, the same way I'd approach a real investigation at work. Over five days I worked through memory forensics, network traffic analysis, and malware analysis challenges with my team, finishing in the top 15% out of 5,600+ teams competing worldwide.

## Dragos CTF

This one moves away from standard IT security entirely and into Operational Technology and Industrial Control Systems — the kind of infrastructure running power grids, manufacturing plants, and critical facilities. The 48-hour format covers ICS protocol analysis, OT-specific malware, and threat intelligence built around how attackers actually target industrial environments, which is a very different mindset from typical enterprise security. It gave me hands-on exposure to a niche that's only going to matter more as critical infrastructure keeps becoming a bigger target.
